Select a photo or screenshot containing the QR Code.
How to use:

Camera:
1. Press START CAMERA.
2. Allow camera permission.
3. Point the camera at the QR Code.
4. The QR value will be sent automatically.

Upload:
1. Press UPLOAD QR IMAGE.
2. Select a QR photo or screenshot.
3. The QR value will be read automatically.
4. It will be sent to the Google Apps Script.
